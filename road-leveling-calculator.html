Road Leveling & RL Calculator

Calculate centre RL between two design chainages and estimate RL at an offset using signed camber.

Calculator


Enter values and tap Calculate.

How it works

Centre RL is linearly interpolated between two known design RL points.

Centre RL = RL1 + ((Target − Ch1) / (Ch2 − Ch1)) × (RL2 − RL1)
Offset RL = Centre RL + Offset × (Camber / 100)

Use a negative camber when RL falls away from the centre/reference line.
